📐 Part 1: Clinical Indices & Mathematical Formulas

1. Cranial Index (CI) - Proportions (Brachycephaly / Scaphocephaly)

Calculated by dividing maximal Biparietal Diameter (BPD) by Occipitofrontal Diameter (OFD):

\text{CI} = \frac{\text{Biparietal Diameter (BPD)}}{\text{Occipitofrontal Diameter (OFD)}}
  • Scaphocephaly / Dolichocephaly: CI < 0.75 (< 75.00%)
  • Mesocephaly (Normal): 0.75 ≤ CI ≤ 0.85 (75.00% – 85.00%)
  • Brachycephaly: CI > 0.85 (> 85.00%)
  • Severe Brachycephaly: CI > 0.90 (> 90.00%)

2. Cranial Vault Asymmetry Index (CVAI) - Dynamic Denominator Option

Calculated from Shorter diagonal diameter and Longer diagonal diameter taken at 30° to the midline sagittal axis:

\text{CVAI (\%)} = \frac{|\text{Longer Diagonal} - \text{Shorter Diagonal}|}{\text{Chosen Denominator Diagonal}} \times 100
  • Standard Denominator (Shorter Diagonal): Default convention used for standard clinical threshold bands.
  • Alternative Denominator (Longer Diagonal): Available via real-time user selection dropdown.
  • Normal Symmetry: CVAI < 3.5%
  • Mild Plagiocephaly: 3.5% ≤ CVAI < 6.25%
  • Moderate Plagiocephaly: 6.25% ≤ CVAI ≤ 8.75%
  • Severe Plagiocephaly: CVAI > 8.75%
  • CVA (Unnormalised Difference): \text{CVA} = |\text{Longer} - \text{Shorter}| (in mm/cm)
Denominator Literature Reference & Finding:
Regardless of whether the shorter or longer diagonal diameter is used as the denominator, research demonstrates little practical clinical difference in threshold values.
Miyabayashi H, Saito K, Kato R, Noto T, Nagano N, Morioka I. Denominator of Cranial Vault Asymmetry Index: Choosing Between Longer and Shorter Diagonal Lengths. J Craniofac Surg. 2023;33(5):e369. doi:10.1097/SCS.0000000000009263
Mandatory Clinical Threshold Note: The conventional cut-off thresholds above were derived using the shorter diagonal diameter as the denominator. Diagnosis is clinical; values can vary depending on age, sex, and race/ethnicity.

3. WHO Head Circumference Centiles & Growth Chart (0 to 60 Months)

Calculates exact Z-scores and centiles for pediatric patients based on the WHO Child Growth Standards (Birth to 5 Years / 60 Months):

Z = \frac{\text{Head Circumference (cm)} - M(\text{age, gender})}{SD(\text{age, gender})}
  • Gender Standards: Separate WHO growth curve datasets for Boys and Girls.
  • Percentile Calculation: Exact standard normal cumulative distribution function ($\Phi(Z)$) outputting centiles from 0.1% to 99.9%.
  • Macrocephaly: Head Circumference > 97th Centile (+2.0 SD)
  • Normal Vault Range: 3rd Centile ≤ Head Circumference ≤ 97th Centile (-2.0 SD to +2.0 SD)
  • Microcephaly: Head Circumference < 3rd Centile (-2.0 SD)
Growth Standard Reference:
World Health Organization. WHO Child Growth Standards: Head circumference-for-age, 0 to 60 completed months. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2006.

⚠️ Part 2: Critical Clinical Points

  • Scaphocephaly Reassurance Alert: A normal CVAI does not reassure in scaphocephaly. Sagittal synostosis is a symmetric deformity, so CVAI remains normal while CI falls.
  • Morphology vs. Metric: CVAI cannot distinguish deformational plagiocephaly (parallelogram deformation) from synostotic plagiocephaly / unicoronal / lambdoid synostosis (trapezoid deformation). Morphological clinical examination is required.
